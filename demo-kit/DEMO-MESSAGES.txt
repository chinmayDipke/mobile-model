THE SEQUENCE - each one shows a DIFFERENT
headline on the block screen
###########################################

--- 1. CASHBACK QR SCAM ---
You have won Rs5000 cashback. Scan the QR to receive the amount in your account.

--- 2. FAKE KYC LINK ---
Dear Customer, your account KYC has EXPIRED. Account will be blocked within 24hrs. Update now: sbi-kyc-verify.in/update

--- 3. REFUND THAT TAKES MONEY ---
You have a COLLECT REQUEST of Rs.4999 from MERCHANT REFUND. Approve to receive your refund amount.

--- 4. FAKE CUSTOMER CARE ---
Your recent transaction of Rs.24999 failed. For instant refund call our customer care 9835442621 immediately.

--- 5. WRONG TRANSFER TRICK ---
Sorry bhai, I sent Rs.9500 to your account by mistake. Please return it to my UPI id, family emergency.

--- 6. DIGITAL ARREST - no rule exists for this. THE MODEL catches it. ---
Cyber Cell notice. A money laundering case is registered against your Aadhaar. Join the video call now to verify your identity, or a warrant will be issued.

    Say: "We wrote no rule for this one. Digital arrest is new.
          The rules caught zero. The on-device model caught it."

--- 7. CLEAN - nothing must happen ---
Rs.199 debited from HDFC Bank Card xx7723 at NETFLIX on 03-09-26. Avl limit Rs.48,801.

    Hold still. Two seconds of silence. Then:
    "Nothing happened. That is correct, and it is half the product."


HOW TO USE IT
-------------
Short on time (Eval Round 1, at the table):   1 then 7.  40 seconds.
Judge is interested:                          1, 6, 7.
They want the full range:                     1 through 7.

WATCH OUT
---------
* Same message twice inside 60 s is suppressed on purpose - one WhatsApp
  message fires up to five notifications. To repeat, change a word
  (Rs5000 -> Rs7000) or send a different one.
* Number 6 goes through the model, so it takes about a second where the
  others are instant. Say so: "that one is slower because the model is
  reading it - the rules are instant, the model understands."

NOTES
=========================================

- WhatsApp is the delivery path. The carrier blocks phishing-shaped SMS
  from personal numbers - our KYC test message never arrived. Do not
  rely on SMS on stage.

- Do NOT have the chat open on the loaner. No notification, no detection.

- The QR beat is the aeroplane-mode beat. A message needs a radio; a QR
  is physically in front of the camera, so only that part is genuinely
  offline. Do not pretend otherwise.

- Loaner volume UP. The warning is spoken.
